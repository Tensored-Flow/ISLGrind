An integer $a$ is called \emph{friendly} if the equation $(m^{2} + n)(n^{2} + m) = a(m - n)^{3}$ has a solution over the positive integers.

\begin{enumerate}
\item[a)] Prove that there are at least $500$ friendly integers in the set $\{1, 2, \ldots, 2012\}$.
\item[b)] Decide whether $a = 2$ is friendly.
\end{enumerate}
