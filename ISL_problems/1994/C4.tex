There are $n+1$ fixed positions in a row, marked $0$ to $n$ from left to right. Cards numbered $0$ to $n$ are shuffled and dealt, one in each position. The object of the game is to have card $i$ in the $i$th position for $0 \leq i \leq n$. If this has not been achieved, the following move is executed. Determine the smallest $k$ such that the $k$th position is occupied by a card $l > k$. Remove this card, slide all cards from the $(k+1)$st to the $l$th position one place to the right, and replace the card $l$ in the $l$th position.

(a) Prove that the game lasts at most $2^n - 1$ moves.

(b) Prove that there exists a unique initial configuration for which the game lasts exactly $2^n - 1$ moves.
