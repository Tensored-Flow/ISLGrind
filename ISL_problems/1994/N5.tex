For any positive integer $k$, $A_k$ is the subset of $\{k+1, k+2, \ldots, 2k\}$ consisting of all elements whose digits in base 2 contain exactly three 1's. Let $f(k)$ denote the number of elements in $A_k$.
(a) Prove that for any positive integer $m$, $f(k) = m$ has at least one solution.
(b) Determine all positive integers $m$ for which $f(k) = m$ has a unique solution.
