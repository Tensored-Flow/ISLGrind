Let $n \geq 3$ be a positive integer. Let $C_1, C_2, C_3, \ldots, C_n$ be unit circles in the plane, with centers $O_1, O_2, O_3, \ldots, O_n$ respectively. If no line meets more than two of the circles, prove that
\[ \sum_{1 \leq i < j \leq n} \frac{1}{O_iO_j} \leq \frac{(n-1)\pi}{4}. \]
