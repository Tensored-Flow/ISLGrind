Let $A$ be a nonempty set of positive integers. Suppose that there are positive integers $b_1, \ldots, b_n$ and $c_1, \ldots, c_n$ such that

(i) for each $i$ the set $b_i A + c_i = \{ b_i a + c_i \mid a \in A \}$ is a subset of $A$, and

(ii) the sets $b_i A + c_i$ and $b_j A + c_j$ are disjoint whenever $i \neq j$.

Prove that
\[ \frac{1}{b_1} + \cdots + \frac{1}{b_n} \leq 1. \]
