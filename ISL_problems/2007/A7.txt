Let n > 1 be an integer. In the space, consider the set
                          S = (x, y, z) | x, y, z ∈ {0, 1, . . . , n}, x + y + z > 0 .

Find the smallest number of planes that jointly contain all (n + 1)3 − 1 points of S but none of
them passes through the origin.
                                                                                   (Netherlands)
Answer. 3n planes.
