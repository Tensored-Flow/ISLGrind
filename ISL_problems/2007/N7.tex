For a prime $p$ and a positive integer $n$, denote by $\nu_p(n)$ the exponent of $p$ in the prime factorization of $n!$. Given a positive integer $d$ and a finite set $\{p_1, \ldots, p_k\}$ of primes. Show that there are infinitely many positive integers $n$ such that $d \mid \nu_{p_i}(n)$ for all $1 \leq i \leq k$.
