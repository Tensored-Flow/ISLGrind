Consider those functions f : N → N which satisfy the condition
                                  f (m + n) ≥ f (m) + f f (n) − 1                                 (1)
for all m, n ∈ N. Find all possible values of f (2007).
    (N denotes the set of all positive integers.)
                                                                                          (Bulgaria)
Answer. 1, 2, . . . , 2008.
