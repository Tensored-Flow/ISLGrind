Let $\nu$ be an irrational positive number, and let $m$ be a positive integer. A pair $(a, b)$ of positive integers is called \emph{good} if
\[ a\lfloor b\nu \rfloor - b\lfloor a\nu \rfloor = m. \]
A good pair $(a, b)$ is called \emph{excellent} if neither of the pairs $(a-b, b)$ and $(a, b-a)$ is good. (As usual, by $\lfloor x \rfloor$ and $\lceil x \rceil$ we denote the integer numbers such that $x - 1 < \lfloor x \rfloor \leq x$ and $x \leq \lceil x \rceil < x + 1$.)

Prove that the number of excellent pairs is equal to the sum of the positive divisors of $m$.
