Let n be a positive integer and let a1 , . . . , an−1 be arbitrary real numbers. Define the
sequences u0 , . . . , un and v0 , . . . , vn inductively by u0 = u1 = v0 = v1 = 1, and
               uk+1 = uk + ak uk−1 ,      vk+1 = vk + an−k vk−1          for k = 1, . . . , n − 1.
Prove that un = vn .
