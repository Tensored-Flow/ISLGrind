Let m ‰ 0 be an integer. Find all polynomials P pxq with real coefficients such that
             px3 − mx2 + 1qP px + 1q + px3 + mx2 + 1qP px − 1q = 2px3 − mx + 1qP pxq
for all real numbers x.
