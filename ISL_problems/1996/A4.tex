Let $a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_n$ be nonnegative real numbers, not all zero.
(a) Prove that $x^n - a_1 x^{n-1} - \cdots - a_{n-1} x - a_n = 0$ has precisely one positive real root.
(b) Let $A = \sum_{j=1}^{n} a_j$, $B = \sum_{j=1}^{n} j a_j$, and let $R$ be the positive real root of the equation in part (a). Prove that
\[
A^A \leq R^B.
\]
