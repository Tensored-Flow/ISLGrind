Let $ABCDEF$ be a convex hexagon such that $AB$ is parallel to $DE$, $BC$ is parallel to $EF$, and $CD$ is parallel to $AF$. Let $R_A, R_C, R_E$ be the circumradii of triangles $FAB, BCD, DEF$ respectively, and let $P$ denote the perimeter of the hexagon. Prove that
\[ R_A + R_C + R_E \geq \frac{P}{2}. \]
