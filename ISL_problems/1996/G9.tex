In the plane are given a point $O$ and a polygon $\mathcal{F}$ (not necessarily convex). Let $P$ denote the perimeter of $\mathcal{F}$, $D$ the sum of the distances from $O$ to the vertices of $\mathcal{F}$, and $H$ the sum of the distances from $O$ to the lines containing the sides of $\mathcal{F}$. Prove that
\[ D^2 - H^2 \geq \frac{P^2}{4}. \]
