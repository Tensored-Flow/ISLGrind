Four integers are marked on a circle. At each step we simultaneously replace each number by the difference between this number and the next number on the circle, in a given direction (that is, the numbers $a, b, c, d$ are replaced by $a - b, b - c, c - d, d - a$). Is it possible after 1996 such steps to have numbers $a, b, c, d$ such that the numbers $|bc - ad|$, $|ac - bd|$, $|ab - cd|$ are primes?
