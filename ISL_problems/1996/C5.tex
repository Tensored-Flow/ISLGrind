Let $p, q, n$ be three positive integers with $p + q < n$. Let $(x_0, x_1, \ldots, x_n)$ be an $(n+1)$-tuple of integers satisfying the following conditions:
(i) $x_0 = x_n = 0$.
(ii) For each $i$ with $1 \leq i \leq n$, either $x_i - x_{i-1} = p$ or $x_i - x_{i-1} = -q$.
Show that there exists a pair $(i, j)$ of distinct indices with $(i, j) \neq (0, n)$ such that $x_i = x_j$.
