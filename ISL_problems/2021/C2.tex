Let $n \geq 3$ be an integer. An integer $m \geq n + 1$ is called $n$-colourful if, given infinitely many marbles in each of $n$ colours $C_1, C_2, \ldots, C_n$, it is possible to place $m$ of them around a circle so that in any group of $n + 1$ consecutive marbles there is at least one marble of colour $C_i$ for each $i = 1, \ldots, n$.

Prove that there are only finitely many positive integers which are not $n$-colourful. Find the largest among them.
