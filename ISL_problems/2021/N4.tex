Alice is given a rational number $r > 1$ and a line with two points $B \neq R$, where point $B$ contains a red bead and point $R$ contains a blue bead. Alice plays a solitaire game by performing a sequence of moves. In every move, she chooses a (not necessarily positive) integer $k$, and a bead to move. If that bead is placed at point $X$, and the other bead is placed at $Y$, then Alice moves the chosen bead to point $X'$ with $\overrightarrow{YX'} = r^k \overrightarrow{YX}$.

Alice's goal is to move the red bead to the point $R$. Find all rational numbers $r > 1$ such that Alice can reach her goal in at most 2021 moves.
