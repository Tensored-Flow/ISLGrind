A point $D$ is chosen inside an acute-angled triangle $ABC$ with $AB > AC$ so that $\angle BAD = \angle DAC$. A point $E$ is constructed on the segment $AC$ so that $\angle ADE = \angle DCB$. Similarly, a point $F$ is constructed on the segment $AB$ so that $\angle ADF = \angle DBC$. A point $X$ is chosen on the line $AC$ so that $CX = BX$. Let $O_1$ and $O_2$ be the circumcentres of the triangles $ADC$ and $DXE$. Prove that the lines $BC$, $EF$, and $O_1 O_2$ are concurrent.
