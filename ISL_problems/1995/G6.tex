Let $A_1A_2A_3A_4$ be a tetrahedron, $G$ its centroid, and $A_1'$, $A_2'$, $A_3'$, and $A_4'$ the points where the circumsphere of $A_1A_2A_3A_4$ intersects $GA_1$, $GA_2$, $GA_3$, and $GA_4$, respectively. Prove that
\[ GA_1 \cdot GA_2 \cdot GA_3 \cdot GA_4 \leq GA_1' \cdot GA_2' \cdot GA_3' \cdot GA_4' \]
and
\[ \frac{1}{GA_1'} + \frac{1}{GA_2'} + \frac{1}{GA_3'} + \frac{1}{GA_4'} \leq \frac{1}{GA_1} + \frac{1}{GA_2} + \frac{1}{GA_3} + \frac{1}{GA_4}. \]
