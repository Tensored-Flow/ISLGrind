Let $ABCDEF$ be a convex hexagon with $AB = BC = CD$, $DE = EF = FA$, and $\angle BCD = \angle EFA = \pi/3$ (that is, $60^\circ$). Let $G$ and $H$ be two points interior to the hexagon such that angles $AGB$ and $DHE$ are both $2\pi/3$ (that is, $120^\circ$). Prove that $AG + GB + GH + DH + HE \geq CF$.
