Determine all integers $n > 3$ such that there are $n$ points $A_1, A_2, \ldots, A_n$ in the plane that satisfy the following two conditions simultaneously:

(a) No three lie on the same line.

(b) There exist real numbers $p_1, p_2, \ldots, p_n$ such that the area of $\triangle A_i A_j A_k$ is equal to $p_i + p_j + p_k$, for $1 \leq i < j < k \leq n$.
