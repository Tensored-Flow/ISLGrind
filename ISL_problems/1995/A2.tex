Let $a$ and $b$ be nonnegative integers such that $ab \geq c^2$, where $c$ is an integer. Prove that there is a number $n$ and integers $x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_n, y_1, y_2, \ldots, y_n$ such that
\[ \sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i^2 = a, \qquad \sum_{i=1}^{n} y_i^2 = b, \qquad \text{and} \qquad \sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i y_i = c. \]
