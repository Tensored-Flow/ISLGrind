Does there exist a sequence $F(1), F(2), F(3), \ldots$ of nonnegative integers that simultaneously satisfies the following three conditions?

(a) Each of the integers $0, 1, 2, \ldots$ occurs in the sequence.

(b) Each positive integer occurs in the sequence infinitely often.

(c) For any $n \geq 2$,
\[ F\left(F(n^{163})\right) = F(F(n)) + F(F(361)). \]
