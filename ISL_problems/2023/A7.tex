Let $N$ be a positive integer. Prove that there exist three permutations $a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_N$; $b_1, b_2, \ldots, b_N$; and $c_1, c_2, \ldots, c_N$ of $1, 2, \ldots, N$ such that
\[ \left| \sqrt{a_k} + \sqrt{b_k} + \sqrt{c_k} - 2\sqrt{N} \right| < 2023 \]
for every $k = 1, 2, \ldots, N$.
