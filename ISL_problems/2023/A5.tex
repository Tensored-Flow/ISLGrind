Let $a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_{2023}$ be positive integers such that
\begin{itemize}
\item $a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_{2023}$ is a permutation of $1, 2, \ldots, 2023$, and
\item $|a_1 - a_2|, |a_2 - a_3|, \ldots, |a_{2022} - a_{2023}|$ is a permutation of $1, 2, \ldots, 2022$.
\end{itemize}
Prove that $\max(a_1, a_{2023}) \geq 507$.
