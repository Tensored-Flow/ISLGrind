Let $\mathbb{Z}_{>0}$ be the set of positive integers. Determine all functions $f \colon \mathbb{Z}_{>0} \to \mathbb{Z}_{>0}$ such that
\[
f^{bf(a)}(a+1) = (a+1)f(b)
\]
holds for all $a, b \in \mathbb{Z}_{>0}$, where $f^k(n) = f(f(\cdots f(n) \cdots))$ denotes the composition of $f$ with itself $k$ times.
