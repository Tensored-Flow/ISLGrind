Suppose that $s_1, s_2, s_3, \ldots$ is a strictly increasing sequence of positive integers such that the subsequences
\[
s_{s_1}, s_{s_2}, s_{s_3}, \ldots \qquad \text{and} \qquad s_{s_1+1}, s_{s_2+1}, s_{s_3+1}, \ldots
\]
are both arithmetic progressions. Prove that $s_1, s_2, s_3, \ldots$ is itself an arithmetic progression.
