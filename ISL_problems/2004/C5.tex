Let $N$ be a positive integer. Two players $A$ and $B$, taking turns, write numbers from the set $\{1, \ldots, N\}$ on a blackboard. $A$ begins the game by writing $1$ on his first move. Then, if a player has written $n$ on a certain move, his adversary is allowed to write $n+1$ or $2n$ (provided the number he writes does not exceed $N$). The player who writes $N$ wins. We say that $N$ is of type $A$ or of type $B$ according as $A$ or $B$ has a winning strategy.

(a) Determine whether $N = 2004$ is of type $A$ or of type $B$.

(b) Find the least $N > 2004$ whose type is different from that of $2004$.
