Let $A_1 A_2 \ldots A_n$ be a regular $n$-gon. The points $B_1, \ldots, B_{n-1}$ are defined as follows:
(i) If $i = 1$ or $i = n-1$, then $B_i$ is the midpoint of the side $A_i A_{i+1}$.
(ii) If $i \neq 1$, $i \neq n-1$, and $S$ is the intersection point of $A_1 A_{i+1}$ and $A_n A_i$, then $B_i$ is the intersection point of the bisector of the angle $A_i S A_{i+1}$ with $A_i A_{i+1}$.
Prove the equality
\[ \angle A_1 B_1 A_n + \angle A_1 B_2 A_n + \cdots + \angle A_1 B_{n-1} A_n = 180^\circ. \]
