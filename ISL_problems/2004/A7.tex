Let $a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_n$ be positive real numbers, $n > 1$. Denote by $g_n$ their geometric mean, and by $A_1, A_2, \ldots, A_n$ the sequence of arithmetic means defined by $A_k = \frac{a_1 + a_2 + \cdots + a_k}{k}$, $k = 1, 2, \ldots, n$. Let $G_n$ be the geometric mean of $A_1, A_2, \ldots, A_n$. Prove the inequality
\[ n\sqrt[n]{\frac{G_n}{A_n}} + \frac{g_n}{G_n} \leq n + 1 \]
and establish the cases of equality.
