A cyclic quadrilateral $ABCD$ is given. The lines $AD$ and $BC$ intersect at $E$, with $C$ between $B$ and $E$; the diagonals $AC$ and $BD$ intersect at $F$. Let $M$ be the midpoint of the side $CD$, and let $N \neq M$ be a point on the circumcircle of the triangle $ABM$ such that $AN/BN = AM/BM$. Prove that the points $E$, $F$, and $N$ are collinear.
