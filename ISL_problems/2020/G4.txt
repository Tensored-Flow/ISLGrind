In the plane, there are n ≥ 6 pairwise disjoint disks D1 , D2 , . . . , Dn with radii
R1 ≥ R2 ≥ . . . ≥ Rn . For every i = 1, 2, . . . , n, a point Pi is chosen in disk Di . Let O be an
arbitrary point in the plane. Prove that

                        OP1 + OP2 + . . . + OPn ≥ R6 + R7 + . . . + Rn .

     (A disk is assumed to contain its boundary.)
