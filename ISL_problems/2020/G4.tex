In the plane, there are $n \geq 6$ pairwise disjoint disks $D_1, D_2, \ldots, D_n$ with radii $R_1 \geq R_2 \geq \ldots \geq R_n$. For every $i = 1, 2, \ldots, n$, a point $P_i$ is chosen in disk $D_i$. Let $O$ be an arbitrary point in the plane. Prove that
\[
OP_1 + OP_2 + \ldots + OP_n \geq R_6 + R_7 + \ldots + R_n.
\]

(A disk is assumed to contain its boundary.)
