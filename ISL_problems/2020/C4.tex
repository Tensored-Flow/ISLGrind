The Fibonacci numbers $F_0, F_1, F_2, \ldots$ are defined inductively by $F_0 = 0$, $F_1 = 1$, and $F_{n+1} = F_n + F_{n-1}$ for $n \geqslant 1$. Given an integer $n \geqslant 2$, determine the smallest size of a set $S$ of integers such that for every $k = 2, 3, \ldots, n$ there exist some $x, y \in S$ such that $x - y = F_k$.
