Determine all functions $f \colon \mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{Z}$ such that
\[
f^{x^2+y^2}(a+b) = af(a) + bf(b) \qquad \text{for every } a, b \in \mathbb{Z}.
\]
Here, $f^n$ denotes the $n^{\text{th}}$ iteration of $f$, i.e., $f^0(x) = x$ and $f^{n+1}(x) = f(f^n(x))$ for all $n \geqslant 0$.
