\textit{Version 1.} Let $n$ be a positive integer, and set $N = 2^n$. Determine the smallest real number $a_n$ such that, for all real $x$,
\[
\sqrt[N]{\frac{x^{2N}+1}{2}} \leqslant a_n(x-1)^2 + x.
\]

\textit{Version 2.} For every positive integer $N$, determine the smallest real number $b_N$ such that, for all real $x$,
\[
\sqrt[N]{\frac{x^{2N}+1}{2}} \leqslant b_N(x-1)^2 + x.
\]
