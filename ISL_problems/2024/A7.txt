Let Q be the set of rational numbers. Let f : Q → Q be a function such that the
following property holds: for all x, y P Q,
                    f px + f pyqq = f pxq + y        or         f pf pxq + yq = x + f pyq.
Determine the maximum possible number of elements of tf pxq + f p−xq | x P Qu.
