Let p ‰ q be coprime positive integers. Determine all infinite sequences a1 , a2 , . . . of
positive integers such that the following conditions hold for all n ≥ 1:

                 maxpan , an+1 , . . . , an+p q − minpan , an+1 , . . . , an+p q = p    and
                 maxpan , an+1 , . . . , an+q q − minpan , an+1 , . . . , an+q q = q.
