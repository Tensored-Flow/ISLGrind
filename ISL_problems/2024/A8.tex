Let $p \neq q$ be coprime positive integers. Determine all infinite sequences $a_1, a_2, \ldots$ of positive integers such that the following conditions hold for all $n \geq 1$:
\begin{align*}
\max(a_n, a_{n+1}, \ldots, a_{n+p}) - \min(a_n, a_{n+1}, \ldots, a_{n+p}) &= p \qquad \text{and} \\
\max(a_n, a_{n+1}, \ldots, a_{n+q}) - \min(a_n, a_{n+1}, \ldots, a_{n+q}) &= q.
\end{align*}
