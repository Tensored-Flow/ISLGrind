Let $ABC$ be a triangle with $AB < AC < BC$, and let $D$ be a point in the interior of segment $BC$. Let $E$ be a point on the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$ such that $A$ and $E$ lie on opposite sides of line $BC$ and $\angle BAD = \angle EAC$. Let $I$, $I_B$, $I_C$, $J_B$, and $J_C$ be the incentres of triangles $ABC$, $ABD$, $ADC$, $ABE$, and $AEC$, respectively. Prove that $I_B$, $I_C$, $J_B$, and $J_C$ are concyclic if and only if $AI$, $I_BJ_C$, and $J_BI_C$ concur.
