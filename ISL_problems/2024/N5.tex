Let $\mathcal{S}$ be a finite nonempty set of prime numbers. Let $1 = b_1 < b_2 < \cdots$ be the sequence of all positive integers whose prime divisors all belong to $\mathcal{S}$. Prove that, for all but finitely many positive integers $n$, there exist positive integers $a_1$, $a_2$, $\ldots$, $a_n$ such that
\[
\frac{a_1}{b_1} + \frac{a_2}{b_2} + \cdots + \frac{a_n}{b_n} = \left\lceil \frac{1}{b_1} + \frac{1}{b_2} + \cdots + \frac{1}{b_n} \right\rceil.
\]
