Let ABCD be a quadrilateral with AB parallel to CD and AB < CD. Lines AD
and BC intersect at a point P . Point X ‰ C on the circumcircle of triangle ABC is such
that P C = P X. Point Y ‰ D on the circumcircle of triangle ABD is such that P D = P Y .
Lines AX and BY intersect at Q.
   Prove that P Q is parallel to AB.
