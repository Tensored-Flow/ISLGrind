Let $N$ be a positive integer and let $a_1, a_2, \ldots$ be an infinite sequence of positive integers. Suppose that, for each $n > N$, $a_n$ is equal to the number of times $a_{n-1}$ appears in the list $a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_{n-1}$.

Prove that at least one of the sequences $a_1, a_3, a_5, \ldots$ and $a_2, a_4, a_6, \ldots$ is eventually periodic.
