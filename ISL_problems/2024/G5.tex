Let $ABC$ be a triangle with incentre $I$, and let $\Omega$ be the circumcircle of triangle $BIC$. Let $K$ be a point in the interior of segment $BC$ such that $\angle BAK < \angle KAC$. The angle bisector of $\angle AKB$ intersects $\Omega$ at points $W$ and $X$ such that $A$ and $W$ lie on the same side of $BC$, and the angle bisector of $\angle CKA$ intersects $\Omega$ at points $Y$ and $Z$ such that $A$ and $Y$ lie on the same side of $BC$. Prove that $\angle WAY = \angle ZAX$.
