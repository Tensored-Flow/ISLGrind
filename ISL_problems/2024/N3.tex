Determine all sequences $a_1, a_2, \ldots$ of positive integers such that, for any pair of positive integers $m \leq n$, the arithmetic and geometric means
\[ \frac{a_m + a_{m+1} + \cdots + a_n}{n - m + 1} \qquad \text{and} \qquad \left(a_m a_{m+1} \cdots a_n\right)^{\frac{1}{n-m+1}} \]
are both integers.
