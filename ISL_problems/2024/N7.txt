Let Z>0 denote the set of positive integers. Let f : Z>0 → Z>0 be a function satisfying
the following property: for m, n P Z>0 , the equation

                               f pmnq2 = f pm2 qf pf pnqqf pmf pnqq

holds if and only if m and n are coprime.
   For each positive integer n, determine all the possible values of f pnq.

 A1.     Determine all real numbers α such that the number

                                      tαu + t2αu + ··· + tnαu

is a multiple of n for every positive integer n. (Here tzu denotes the greatest integer less than
or equal to z.)
                                                                                     (Colombia)

Answer: All even integers satisfy the condition of the problem and no other real number α
does so.
