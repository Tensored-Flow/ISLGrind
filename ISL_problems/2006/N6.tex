Let $a > b > 1$ be relatively prime positive integers. Define the \emph{weight} of an integer $c$, denoted by $w(c)$, to be the minimal possible value of $|x| + |y|$ taken over all pairs of integers $x$ and $y$ such that
\[
ax + by = c.
\]
An integer $c$ is called a \emph{local champion} if $w(c) \geq w(c \pm a)$ and $w(c) \geq w(c \pm b)$.

Find all local champions and determine their number.
