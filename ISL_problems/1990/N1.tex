The integer 9 can be written as a sum of two consecutive integers: $9 = 4 + 5$. Moreover, it can be written as a sum of (more than one) consecutive positive integers in exactly two ways: $9 = 4 + 5 = 2 + 3 + 4$. Is there an integer that can be written as a sum of 1990 consecutive positive integers and that can be written as a sum of (more than one) consecutive positive integers in exactly 1990 ways?
