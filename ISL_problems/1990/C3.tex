Two players $A$ and $B$ play a game in which they choose numbers alternately according to the following rule: At the beginning, an initial natural number $n_0 > 1$ is given. Knowing $n_{2k}$, player $A$ may choose any $n_{2k+1} \in \mathbb{N}$ such that
\[ n_{2k} \leq n_{2k+1} \leq n_{2k}^2. \]
Then player $B$ chooses a number $n_{2k+2} \in \mathbb{N}$ such that
\[ \frac{n_{2k+1}}{n_{2k+2}} = p^r, \]
where $p$ is a prime number and $r \in \mathbb{N}$. It is stipulated that player $A$ wins the game if he (she) succeeds in choosing the number 1990, and player $B$ wins if he (she) succeeds in choosing 1. For which natural numbers $n_0$ can player $A$ manage to win the game, for which $n_0$ can player $B$ manage to win, and for which $n_0$ can players $A$ and $B$ each force a tie?
