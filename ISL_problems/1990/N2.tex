Assume that the set of all positive integers is decomposed into $r$ (disjoint) subsets $A_1 \cup A_2 \cup \cdots A_r = \mathbb{N}$. Prove that one of them, say $A_i$, has the following property: There exists a positive $m$ such that for any $k$ one can find numbers $a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_k$ in $A_i$ with $0 < a_{j+1} - a_j \leq m$ ($1 \leq j \leq k - 1$).
