Consider an infinite sequence $a_1, a_2, \ldots$ of positive integers with $a_i \leq 2015$ for all $i \geq 1$. Suppose that for any two distinct indices $i$ and $j$ we have $i + a_i \neq j + a_j$.

Prove that there exist two positive integers $b$ and $N$ such that
\[ \left| \sum_{i=m+1}^{n} (a_i - b) \right| \leq 1007^2 \]
whenever $n > m \geq N$.
