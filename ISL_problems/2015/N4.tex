Suppose that $a_0, a_1, \ldots$ and $b_0, b_1, \ldots$ are two sequences of positive integers satisfying $a_0, b_0 \geq 2$ and
\[
a_{n+1} = \gcd(a_n, b_n) + 1, \qquad b_{n+1} = \operatorname{lcm}(a_n, b_n) - 1
\]
for all $n \geq 0$. Prove that the sequence $(a_n)$ is eventually periodic; in other words, there exist integers $N > 0$ and $t > 0$ such that $a_{n+t} = a_n$ for all $n \geq N$.
