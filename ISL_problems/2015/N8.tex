For every positive integer $n$ with prime factorization $n = \prod_{i=1}^{k} p_i^{\alpha_i}$, define
\[
\mho(n) = \sum_{i \colon p_i > 10^{100}} \alpha_i.
\]
That is, $\mho(n)$ is the number of prime factors of $n$ greater than $10^{100}$, counted with multiplicity. Find all strictly increasing functions $f \colon \mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{Z}$ such that
\[
\mho\!\left(f(a) - f(b)\right) \leq \mho(a - b) \qquad \text{for all integers } a \text{ and } b \text{ with } a > b.
\]
