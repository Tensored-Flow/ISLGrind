For every positive integer n with prime factorization n = i=1 pi , define
                                     ℧pnq =            αi .
                                                      i : pi >10100

That is, ℧pnq is the number of prime factors of n greater than 10100 , counted with multiplicity.
  Find all strictly increasing functions f : Z → Z such that
              ℧ f paq − f pbq ≤ ℧pa − bq       for all integers a and b with a > b.
