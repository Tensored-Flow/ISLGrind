Let $I$ be the incenter of a triangle $ABC$ and $\Gamma$ be its circumcircle. Let the line $AI$ intersect $\Gamma$ at a point $D \neq A$. Let $F$ and $E$ be points on side $BC$ and arc $BDC$ respectively such that $\angle BAF = \angle CAE < \frac{1}{2}\angle BAC$. Finally, let $G$ be the midpoint of the segment $IF$. Prove that the lines $DG$ and $EI$ intersect on $\Gamma$.
