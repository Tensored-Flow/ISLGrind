$n \geq 4$ players participated in a tennis tournament. Any two players have played exactly one game, and there was no tie game. We call a company of four players \emph{bad} if one player was defeated by the other three players, and each of these three players won a game and lost another game among themselves. Suppose that there is no bad company in this tournament. Let $w_i$ and $\ell_i$ be respectively the number of wins and losses of the $i$th player. Prove that
\[
\sum_{i=1}^{n} (w_i - \ell_i)^3 \geq 0. \tag{1}
\]
