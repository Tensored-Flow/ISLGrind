Six stacks $S_1, \ldots, S_6$ of coins are standing in a row. In the beginning every stack contains a single coin. There are two types of allowed moves:

\emph{Move 1:} If stack $S_k$ with $1 \leq k \leq 5$ contains at least one coin, you may remove one coin from $S_k$ and add two coins to $S_{k+1}$.

\emph{Move 2:} If stack $S_k$ with $1 \leq k \leq 4$ contains at least one coin, then you may remove one coin from $S_k$ and exchange stacks $S_{k+1}$ and $S_{k+2}$.

Decide whether it is possible to achieve by a sequence of such moves that the first five stacks are empty, whereas the sixth stack $S_6$ contains exactly $2010^{2010^{2010}}$ coins.
