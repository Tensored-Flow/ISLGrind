(a) Show that the set $\mathbb{Q}^{+}$ of all positive rational numbers can be partitioned into three disjoint subsets $A,B,C$ satisfying the following conditions:
\[
BA=B,\quad B^{2}=C,\quad BC=A,
\]
where $HK$ stands for the set $\{hk\mid h\in H, k\in K\}$ for any two subsets $H,K$ of $\mathbb{Q}^{+}$ and $H^{2}$ stands for $HH$.
(b) Show that all positive rational cubes are in $A$ for such a partition of $\mathbb{Q}^{+}$.
(c) Find such a partition $\mathbb{Q}^{+}=A\cup B\cup C$ with the property that for no positive integer $n\leq 34$ are both $n$ and $n+1$ in $A$; that is,
\[
\min\{n\in\mathbb{N}\mid n\in A, n+1\in A\}>34.
\]
