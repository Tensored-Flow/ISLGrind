Let $a, b, c$ be given integers $a > 0$, $ac - b^2 = P = P_1 \cdots P_n$, where $P_1, \ldots, P_n$ are (distinct) prime numbers. Let $M(n)$ denote the number of pairs of integers $(x, y)$ for which
\[ ax^2 + 2bxy + cy^2 = n. \]
Prove that $M(n)$ is finite and $M(n) = M(P^k \cdot n)$ for every integer $k \geq 0$.
