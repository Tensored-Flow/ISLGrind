Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $n \geq 2$, and $A_0 = (a_{01}, a_{02}, \ldots, a_{0n})$ be any $n$-tuple of natural numbers such that $0 \leq a_{0i} \leq i-1$, for $i = 1, \ldots, n$. The $n$-tuples $A_1 = (a_{11}, a_{12}, \ldots, a_{1n})$, $A_2 = (a_{21}, a_{22}, \ldots, a_{2n}), \ldots$ are defined by
\[ a_{i+1,j} = \mathrm{Card}\{a_{i,l} \mid 1 \leq l \leq j-1, a_{i,l} \geq a_{i,j}\}, \quad \text{for } i \in \mathbb{N} \text{ and } j = 1, \ldots, n. \]
Prove that there exists $k \in \mathbb{N}$, such that $A_{k+2} = A_k$.
