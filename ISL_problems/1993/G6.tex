A circle $S$ is said to cut a circle $\Sigma$ \emph{diametrically} if their common chord is a diameter of $\Sigma$. Let $S_A, S_B, S_C$ be three circles with distinct centers $A, B, C$ respectively. Prove that $A, B, C$ are collinear if and only if there is no unique circle $S$ that cuts each of $S_A, S_B, S_C$ diametrically. Prove further that if there exists more than one circle $S$ that cuts each of $S_A, S_B, S_C$ diametrically, then all such circles pass through two fixed points. Locate these points in relation to the circles $S_A, S_B, S_C$.
