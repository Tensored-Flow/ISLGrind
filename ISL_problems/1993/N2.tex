Define a sequence $\langle f(n)\rangle_{n=1}^{\infty}$ of positive integers by $f(1)=1$ and
\[
f(n)=\begin{cases} f(n-1)-n, & \text{if } f(n-1)>n; \\ f(n-1)+n, & \text{if } f(n-1)\leq n, \end{cases}
\]
for $n\geq 2$. Let $S=\{n\in\mathbb{N}\mid f(n)=1993\}$.
(a) Prove that $S$ is an infinite set.
(b) Find the least positive integer in $S$.
(c) If all the elements of $S$ are written in ascending order as $n_1<n_2<n_3<\cdots$, show that
\[
\lim_{i\to\infty}\frac{n_{i+1}}{n_i}=3.
\]
