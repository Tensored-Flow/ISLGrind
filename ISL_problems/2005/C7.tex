Let $n \geq 1$ be a given integer, and let $a_1, \ldots, a_n$ be a sequence of integers such that $n$ divides the sum $a_1 + \cdots + a_n$. Show that there exist permutations $\sigma$ and $\tau$ of $1, 2, \ldots, n$ such that $\sigma(i) + \tau(i) \equiv a_i \pmod{n}$ for all $i = 1, \ldots, n$.
