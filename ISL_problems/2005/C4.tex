Let $n \geq 3$ be a given positive integer. We wish to label each side and each diagonal of a regular $n$-gon $P_1 \ldots P_n$ with a positive integer less than or equal to $r$ so that:
\begin{itemize}
\item[(i)] every integer between $1$ and $r$ occurs as a label;
\item[(ii)] in each triangle $P_iP_jP_k$ two of the labels are equal and greater than the third.
\end{itemize}
Given these conditions:
\begin{itemize}
\item[(a)] Determine the largest positive integer $r$ for which this can be done.
\item[(b)] For that value of $r$, how many such labelings are there?
\end{itemize}
