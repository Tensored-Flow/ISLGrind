A sequence of real numbers $a_0, a_1, a_2, \ldots$ is defined by the formula
\[ a_{i+1} = \lfloor a_i \rfloor \cdot \{a_i\}, \quad \text{for } i \geq 0; \]
here $a_0$ is an arbitrary number, $\lfloor a_i \rfloor$ denotes the greatest integer not exceeding $a_i$, and $\{a_i\} = a_i - \lfloor a_i \rfloor$. Prove that $a_i = a_{i+2}$ for $i$ sufficiently large.
