Six points are chosen on the sides of an equilateral triangle $ABC$: $A_1, A_2$ on $BC$; $B_1, B_2$ on $CA$; $C_1, C_2$ on $AB$. These points are vertices of a convex hexagon $A_1 A_2 B_1 B_2 C_1 C_2$ with equal side lengths. Prove that the lines $A_1 B_2$, $B_1 C_2$ and $C_1 A_2$ are concurrent.
