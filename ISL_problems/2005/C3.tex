In an $m \times n$ rectangular board of $mn$ unit squares, \emph{adjacent} squares are ones with a common edge, and a \emph{path} is a sequence of squares in which any two consecutive squares are adjacent. Each square of the board can be colored black or white. Let $N$ denote the number of colorings of the board such that there exists at least one black path from the left edge of the board to its right edge, and let $M$ denote the number of colorings in which there exist at least two nonintersecting black paths from the left edge to the right edge. Prove that $N^2 \geq 2^{mn}M$.
