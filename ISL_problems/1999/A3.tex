A game is played by $n$ girls ($n \geq 2$), everybody having a ball. Each of the $\binom{n}{2}$ pairs of players, in an arbitrary order, exchange the balls they have at that moment. The game is called \emph{nice} if at the end nobody has her own ball, and it is called \emph{tiresome} if at the end everybody has her initial ball. Determine the values of $n$ for which there exists a nice game and those for which there exists a tiresome game.
