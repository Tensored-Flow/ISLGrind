Denote by $S$ the set of all primes $p$ such that the decimal representation of $1/p$ has the fundamental period divisible by 3. For every $p \in S$ such that $1/p$ has its fundamental period $3r$ one may write $1/p = 0.\overline{a_1 a_2 \ldots a_{3r} a_1 a_2 \ldots a_{3r} \ldots}$, where $r = r(p)$; for every $p \in S$ and every integer $k \geq 1$ define $f(k, p)$ by
\[ f(k, p) = a_k + a_{k+r(p)} + a_{k+2r(p)}. \]
(a) Prove that $S$ is infinite.
(b) Find the highest value of $f(k, p)$ for $k \geq 1$ and $p \in S$.
