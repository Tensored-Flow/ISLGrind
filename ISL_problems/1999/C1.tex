Let $n \geq 1$ be an integer. A \emph{path} from $(0,0)$ to $(n,n)$ in the $xy$ plane is a chain of consecutive unit moves either to the right (move denoted by $E$) or upwards (move denoted by $N$), all the moves being made inside the half-plane $x \geq y$. A \emph{step} in a path is the occurrence of two consecutive moves of the form $EN$.

Show that the number of paths from $(0,0)$ to $(n,n)$ that contain exactly $s$ steps ($n \geq s \geq 1$) is
\[ \frac{1}{s} \binom{n-1}{s-1} \binom{n}{s-1}. \]
