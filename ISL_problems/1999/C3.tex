A biologist watches a chameleon. The chameleon catches flies and rests after each catch. The biologist notices that:
\begin{itemize}
\item[(i)] the first fly is caught after a resting period of one minute;
\item[(ii)] the resting period before catching the $2m$th fly is the same as the resting period before catching the $m$th fly and one minute shorter than the resting period before catching the $(2m+1)$th fly;
\item[(iii)] when the chameleon stops resting, he catches a fly instantly.
\end{itemize}
(a) How many flies were caught by the chameleon before his first resting period of 9 minutes?

(b) After how many minutes will the chameleon catch his 98th fly?

(c) How many flies were caught by the chameleon after 1999 minutes passed?
