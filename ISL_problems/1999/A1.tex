Let $n \geq 2$ be a fixed integer. Find the least constant $C$ such that the inequality
\[ \sum_{i<j} x_i x_j (x_i^2 + x_j^2) \leq C \left( \sum_i x_i \right)^4 \]
holds for every $x_1, \ldots, x_n \geq 0$ (the sum on the left consists of $\binom{n}{2}$ summands). For this constant $C$, characterize the instances of equality.
