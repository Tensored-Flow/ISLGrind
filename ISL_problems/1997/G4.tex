Let $A_1 A_2 A_3$ be a nonisosceles triangle with incenter $I$. Let $C_i$, $i = 1, 2, 3$, be the smaller circle through $I$ tangent to $A_i A_{i+1}$ and $A_i A_{i+2}$ (the addition of indices being mod 3). Let $B_i$, $i = 1, 2, 3$, be the second point of intersection of $C_{i+1}$ and $C_{i+2}$. Prove that the circumcenters of the triangles $A_1 B_1 I$, $A_2 B_2 I$, $A_3 B_3 I$ are collinear.
