Let $x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_n$ be real numbers satisfying the conditions \[ |x_1 + x_2 + \cdots + x_n| = 1 \quad \text{and} \quad |x_i| \leq \frac{n+1}{2} \quad \text{for} \quad i = 1, 2, \ldots, n. \] Show that there exists a permutation $y_1, \ldots, y_n$ of the sequence $x_1, \ldots, x_n$ such that \[ |y_1 + 2y_2 + \cdots + ny_n| \leq \frac{n+1}{2}. \]
