Let $n \geq 3$ be an integer, and let $x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_n$ be real numbers in the interval $[0,1]$. Let $s = x_1 + x_2 + \cdots + x_n$, and assume that $s \geq 3$. Prove that there exist integers $i$ and $j$ with $1 \leq i < j \leq n$ such that
\[
2^{j-i} x_i x_j > 2^{s-3}.
\]
