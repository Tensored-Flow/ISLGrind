Let $\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}$ be the set of non-negative integers, and let $f : \mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0} \times \mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0} \to \mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}$ be a bijection such that whenever $f(x_1, y_1) > f(x_2, y_2)$, we have $f(x_1 + 1, y_1) > f(x_2 + 1, y_2)$ and $f(x_1, y_1 + 1) > f(x_2, y_2 + 1)$.

Let $N$ be the number of pairs of integers $(x, y)$, with $0 \leq x, y < 100$, such that $f(x, y)$ is odd. Find the smallest and largest possible value of $N$.
