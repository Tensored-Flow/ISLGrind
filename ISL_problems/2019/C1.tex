The infinite sequence $a_0,\, a_1,\, a_2,\, \ldots$ of (not necessarily different) integers has the following properties: $0 \leq a_i \leq i$ for all integers $i \geq 0$, and
\[
\binom{k}{a_0} + \binom{k}{a_1} + \cdots + \binom{k}{a_k} = 2^k
\]
for all integers $k \geq 0$.

Prove that all integers $N \geq 0$ occur in the sequence (that is, for all $N \geq 0$, there exists $i \geq 0$ with $a_i = N$).
