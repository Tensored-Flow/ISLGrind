Let $u_1, u_2, \ldots, u_{2019}$ be real numbers satisfying
\[ u_1 + u_2 + \cdots + u_{2019} = 0 \quad \text{and} \quad u_1^2 + u_2^2 + \cdots + u_{2019}^2 = 1. \]
Let $a = \min(u_1, u_2, \ldots, u_{2019})$ and $b = \max(u_1, u_2, \ldots, u_{2019})$. Prove that
\[ ab \leq -\frac{1}{2019}. \]
