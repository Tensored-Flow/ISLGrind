Let $n$ be a positive integer. Harry has $n$ coins lined up on his desk, each showing heads or tails. He repeatedly does the following operation: if there are $k$ coins showing heads and $k > 0$, then he flips the $k^{\text{th}}$ coin over; otherwise he stops the process. (For example, the process starting with $THT$ would be $THT \to HHT \to HTT \to TTT$, which takes three steps.)

Letting $C$ denote the initial configuration (a sequence of $n$ $H$'s and $T$'s), write $\ell(C)$ for the number of steps needed before all coins show $T$. Show that this number $\ell(C)$ is finite, and determine its average value over all $2^n$ possible initial configurations $C$.
