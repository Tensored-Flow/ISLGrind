(a) Prove that for every positive integer $n$, there exists a fraction $\frac{a}{b}$ where $a$ and $b$ are integers satisfying $0 < b \leq \sqrt{n} + 1$ and $\sqrt{n} \leq \frac{a}{b} \leq \sqrt{n+1}$.

(b) Prove that there are infinitely many positive integers $n$ such that there is no fraction $\frac{a}{b}$ where $a$ and $b$ are integers satisfying $0 < b \leq \sqrt{n}$ and $\sqrt{n} \leq \frac{a}{b} \leq \sqrt{n+1}$.
