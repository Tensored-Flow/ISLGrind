Find all integers $n \geqslant 3$ with the following property: for all real numbers $a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_n$ and $b_1, b_2, \ldots, b_n$ satisfying $|a_k| + |b_k| = 1$ for $1 \leqslant k \leqslant n$, there exist $x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_n$, each of which is either $-1$ or $1$, such that
\[
\left| \sum_{k=1}^{n} x_k a_k \right| + \left| \sum_{k=1}^{n} x_k b_k \right| \leqslant 1.
\]
