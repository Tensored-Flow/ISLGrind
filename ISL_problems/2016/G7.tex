Let $I$ be the incentre of a non-equilateral triangle $ABC$, $I_A$ be the $A$-excentre, $I_A'$ be the reflection of $I_A$ in $BC$, and $\ell_A$ be the reflection of line $AI_A'$ in $AI$. Define points $I_B$, $I_B'$ and line $\ell_B$ analogously. Let $P$ be the intersection point of $\ell_A$ and $\ell_B$.

\medskip

(a) Prove that $P$ lies on line $OI$ where $O$ is the circumcentre of triangle $ABC$.

\medskip

(b) Let one of the tangents from $P$ to the incircle of triangle $ABC$ meet the circumcircle at points $X$ and $Y$. Show that $\angle XIY = 120^{\circ}$.
