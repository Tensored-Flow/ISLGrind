The sequence $a_0, a_1, a_2, \ldots$ of positive integers satisfies
\[
a_{n+1} = \begin{cases} \sqrt{a_n}, & \text{if } \sqrt{a_n} \text{ is an integer} \\ a_n + 3, & \text{otherwise} \end{cases} \qquad \text{for every } n \geq 0.
\]
Determine all values of $a_0 > 1$ for which there is at least one number $a$ such that $a_n = a$ for infinitely many values of $n$.
