Say that an ordered pair $(x, y)$ of integers is an \emph{irreducible lattice point} if $x$ and $y$ are relatively prime. For any finite set $S$ of irreducible lattice points, show that there is a homogenous polynomial in two variables, $f(x, y)$, with integer coefficients, of degree at least 1, such that $f(x, y) = 1$ for each $(x, y)$ in the set $S$.

\textbf{Note:} A homogenous polynomial of degree $n$ is any nonzero polynomial of the form
\[
f(x, y) = a_0 x^n + a_1 x^{n-1} y + a_2 x^{n-2} y^2 + \cdots + a_{n-1} x y^{n-1} + a_n y^n.
\]
