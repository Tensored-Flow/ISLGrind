Let $D_1, \ldots, D_n$ be closed disks in the plane. (A closed disk is a region bounded by a circle, taken jointly with this circle.) Suppose that every point in the plane is contained in at most $2003$ disks $D_i$. Prove that there exists a disk $D_k$ that intersects at most $7 \cdot 2003 - 1$ other disks $D_i$.
