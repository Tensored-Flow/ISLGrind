Let $n$ be a positive integer and let $(x_1, \ldots, x_n)$, $(y_1, \ldots, y_n)$ be two sequences of positive real numbers. Suppose $(z_2, z_3, \ldots, z_{2n})$ is a sequence of positive real numbers such that
\[ z_{i+j}^2 \geq x_i y_j \quad \text{for all } 1 \leq i, j \leq n. \]
Let $M = \max\{z_2, \ldots, z_{2n}\}$. Prove that
\[ \left( \frac{M + z_2 + \cdots + z_{2n}}{2n} \right)^2 \geq \left( \frac{x_1 + \cdots + x_n}{n} \right) \left( \frac{y_1 + \cdots + y_n}{n} \right). \]
