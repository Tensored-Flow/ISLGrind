Let $n$ be a positive integer and let $x_1 \leq x_2 \leq \cdots \leq x_n$ be real numbers.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that
\[ \left( \sum_{i,j=1}^{n} |x_i - x_j| \right)^2 \leq \frac{2(n^2 - 1)}{3} \sum_{i,j=1}^{n} (x_i - x_j)^2. \]
\item[(b)] Show that equality holds if and only if $x_1, \ldots, x_n$ is an arithmetic progression.
\end{enumerate}
