Let $f(k)$ be the number of integers $n$ that satisfy the following conditions:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] $0 \leq n < 10^k$, so $n$ has exactly $k$ digits (in decimal notation), with leading zeros allowed;
\item[(ii)] the digits of $n$ can be permuted in such a way that they yield an integer divisible by $11$.
\end{enumerate}
Prove that $f(2m) = 10 f(2m - 1)$ for every positive integer $m$.
