Let $f(x)$ be a polynomial with rational coefficients and $\alpha$ be a real number such that $\alpha^3 - \alpha = f(\alpha)^3 - f(\alpha) = 33^{1992}$. Prove that for each $n \geq 1$,
\[ (f^{(n)}(\alpha))^3 - f^{(n)}(\alpha) = 33^{1992}, \]
where $f^{(n)}(x) = f(f(\dots f(x)))$, and $n$ is a positive integer.
