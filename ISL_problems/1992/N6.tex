For each positive integer $n$, denote by $s(n)$ the greatest integer such that for all positive integers $k \leq s(n)$, $n^2$ can be expressed as a sum of squares of $k$ positive integers.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that $s(n) \leq n^2 - 14$ for all $n \geq 4$.
\item[(b)] Find a number $n$ such that $s(n) = n^2 - 14$.
\item[(c)] Prove that there exist infinitely many positive integers $n$ such that $s(n) = n^2 - 14$.
\end{enumerate}
