Let $\mathbb{R}^+$ be the set of all nonnegative real numbers. Given two positive real numbers $a$ and $b$, suppose that a mapping $f : \mathbb{R}^+ \to \mathbb{R}^+$ satisfies the functional equation
\[ f(f(x)) + af(x) = b(a + b)x. \]
Prove that there exists a unique solution of this equation.
