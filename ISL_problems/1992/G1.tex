The diagonals of a quadrilateral $ABCD$ are perpendicular: $AC \perp BD$. Four squares, $ABEF, BCGH, CDIJ, DAKL$, are erected externally on its sides. The intersection points of the pairs of straight lines $CL, DF$; $DF, AH$; $AH, BJ$; $BJ, CL$ are denoted by $P_1, Q_1, R_1, S_1$, respectively, and the intersection points of the pairs of straight lines $AI, BK$; $BK, CE$; $CE, DG$; $DG, AI$ are denoted by $P_2, Q_2, R_2, S_2$, respectively. Prove that $P_1 Q_1 R_1 S_1 \cong P_2 Q_2 R_2 S_2$.
