Let $V$ be a finite subset of Euclidean space consisting of points $(x, y, z)$ with integer coordinates. Let $S_1, S_2, S_3$ be the projections of $V$ onto the $yz$-, $zx$-, $xy$-planes, respectively. Prove that
\[ |V|^2 \leq |S_1| |S_2| |S_3|. \]
($|X|$ denotes the number of elements of $X$.)
