Let $[x]$ denote the greatest integer less than or equal to $x$. Pick any $x_1$ in $[0,1)$ and define the sequence $x_1, x_2, x_3, \ldots$ by $x_{n+1} = 0$ if $x_n = 0$ and $x_{n+1} = 1/x_n - [1/x_n]$ otherwise. Prove that
\[ x_1 + x_2 + \cdots + x_n < \frac{F_1}{F_2} + \frac{F_2}{F_3} + \cdots + \frac{F_n}{F_{n+1}}, \]
where $F_1 = F_2 = 1$ and $F_{n+2} = F_{n+1} + F_n$ for $n \geq 1$.
