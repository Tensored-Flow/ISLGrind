In the plane we are given a set $E$ of 1991 points, and certain pairs of these points are joined with a path. We suppose that for every point of $E$, there exist at least 1593 other points of $E$ to which it is joined by a path. Show that there exist six points of $E$ every pair of which are joined by a path.

\textit{Alternative version.} Is it possible to find a set $E$ of 1991 points in the plane and paths joining certain pairs of the points in $E$ such that every point of $E$ is joined by a path with at least 1592 other points of $E$, and in every subset of six points of $E$ there exist at least two points that are not joined?
