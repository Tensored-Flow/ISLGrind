Given any integer $n \geq 2$, assume that the integers $a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_n$ are not divisible by $n$ and, moreover, that $n$ does not divide $a_1 + a_2 + \cdots + a_n$. Prove that there exist at least $n$ different sequences $(\epsilon_1, \epsilon_2, \cdots, \epsilon_n)$ consisting of zeros or ones such that $\epsilon_1 a_1 + \epsilon_2 a_2 + \cdots + \epsilon_n a_n$ is divisible by $n$.
