We call a set $S$ on the real line $\mathbb{R}$ \emph{superinvariant} if for any stretching $A$ of the set by the transformation taking $x$ to $A(x) = x_0 + a(x - x_0)$ there exists a translation $B$, $B(x) = x + b$, such that the images of $S$ under $A$ and $B$ agree; i.e., for any $x \in S$ there is a $y \in S$ such that $A(x) = B(y)$ and for any $t \in S$ there is a $u \in S$ such that $B(t) = A(u)$. Determine all superinvariant sets.

\emph{Remark.} It is assumed that $a > 0$.
