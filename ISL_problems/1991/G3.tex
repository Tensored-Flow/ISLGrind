Let $S$ be any point on the circumscribed circle of $\triangle PQR$. Then the feet of the perpendiculars from $S$ to the three sides of the triangle lie on the same straight line. Denote this line by $l(S, PQR)$. Suppose that the hexagon $ABCDEF$ is inscribed in a circle. Show that the four lines $l(A, BDF)$, $l(B, ACE)$, $l(C, ABF)$, and $l(E, ABC)$ intersect at one point if and only if $CDEF$ is a rectangle.
