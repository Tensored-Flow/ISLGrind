Let $n \geq 2$ be a natural number and let the real numbers $p, a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_n, b_1, b_2, \ldots, b_n$ satisfy $1/2 \leq p \leq 1$, $0 \leq a_i$, $0 \leq b_i \leq p$, $i = 1, \ldots, n$, and $\sum_{i=1}^{n} a_i = \sum_{i=1}^{n} b_i = 1$. Prove the inequality
\[ \sum_{i=1}^{n} b_i \prod_{\substack{j=1 \\ j \neq i}}^{n} a_j \leq \frac{p}{(n-1)^{n-1}}. \]
