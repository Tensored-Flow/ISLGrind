Let $n\geq 2018$ be an integer, and let $a_1,a_2,\ldots,a_n,b_1,b_2,\ldots,b_n$ be pairwise distinct positive integers not exceeding $5n$. Suppose that the sequence
\[
\frac{a_1}{b_1},\frac{a_2}{b_2},\ldots,\frac{a_n}{b_n}
\]
forms an arithmetic progression. Prove that the terms of the sequence are equal.
