Let $a_1,a_2,\ldots,a_n,\ldots$ be a sequence of positive integers such that
\[
\frac{a_1}{a_2}+\frac{a_2}{a_3}+\cdots+\frac{a_{n-1}}{a_n}+\frac{a_n}{a_1}
\]
is an integer for all $n\geq k$, where $k$ is some positive integer. Prove that there exists a positive integer $m$ such that $a_n=a_{n+1}$ for all $n\geq m$.
