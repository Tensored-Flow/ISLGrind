Let $S = \{x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_{k+\ell}\}$ be a $(k+\ell)$-element set of real numbers contained in the interval $[0,1]$; $k$ and $\ell$ are positive integers. A $k$-element subset $A \subset S$ is called \emph{nice} if
\[
\left| \frac{1}{k} \sum_{x_i \in A} x_i - \frac{1}{\ell} \sum_{x_j \in S \setminus A} x_j \right| \leq \frac{k+\ell}{2k\ell}.
\]
Prove that the number of nice subsets is at least $\dfrac{2}{k+\ell} \dbinom{k+\ell}{k}$.
