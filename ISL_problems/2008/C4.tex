Let $n$ and $k$ be fixed positive integers of the same parity, $k \geq n$. We are given $2n$ lamps numbered $1$ through $2n$; each of them can be on or off. At the beginning all lamps are off. We consider sequences of $k$ steps. At each step one of the lamps is switched (from off to on or from on to off).

Let $N$ be the number of $k$-step sequences ending in the state: lamps $1, \ldots, n$ on, lamps $n+1, \ldots, 2n$ off.

Let $M$ be the number of $k$-step sequences leading to the same state and not touching lamps $n+1, \ldots, 2n$ at all.

Find the ratio $N/M$.
